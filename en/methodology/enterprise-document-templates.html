# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

- Document Code: `EMP-P0-01` | Version: `v2.0`

{{Client Name}} (hereinafter referred to as "Party A") and Empasy Inc. (hereinafter referred to as "Party B") enter into this Agreement as follows to protect confidential information exchanged in connection with the execution of "{{Project Name}}" (hereinafter referred to as the "Engagement").

### Article 1 (Purpose)
The purpose of this Agreement is to protect all technical, business, data, and other confidential information disclosed by either Party to the other during the evaluation, Proof of Concept (PoC), proposal, contracting, and execution of the "Engagement," and to prevent unauthorized external disclosure.

### Article 2 (Definition of Confidential Information)
1. "Confidential Information" means all business plans, architectures, source code, database schemas, LLM prompts and rule dictionaries, cost structures, and client data disclosed or delivered in writing, orally, electronically, or by any other means.
2. Information delivered in writing or electronic files shall be marked "Confidential," and information disclosed orally shall be confirmed as confidential in writing within 14 days of disclosure.

### Article 3 (Confidentiality Obligations & Prohibition of External AI Training)
1. Neither Party may use Confidential Information received from the other for any purpose other than the "Engagement" (including proposals to other clients or training external general-purpose AI/LLM models), nor disclose, distribute, or reproduce it to any third party without the other Party's prior written consent.
2. The receiving Party shall limit disclosure strictly to employees and approved partner personnel with a need to know and shall bind them to equivalent confidentiality obligations.

### Article 4 (Return and Destruction of Information)
Upon expiration of this Agreement or upon the other Party's written request for return, the receiving Party shall permanently destroy all originals, copies, and electronic files by irreversible means within 14 days and issue a certificate of destruction upon request.

### Article 5 (Term and Damages)
1. This Agreement takes effect on the execution date and remains valid for three (3) years from the date Confidential Information is disclosed.
2. A breaching Party shall compensate the other Party for ordinary damages incurred, and the Seoul Central District Court shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
