# Empasy Business & Project Management Standard Document Templates Download Center (125 Templates)

This center provides **125 deduplicated single-standard blank templates (Clean Blank Master)**, **121 real-world filled examples (`SyncFlow AI` Sample)**, and **14 synchronized Markdown (Quick Copy) templates** side by side across the entire lifecycle of **Empasy Business & Development Methodology v2.0 (Phase 0 Pre-sales through Phase 5 Go-Live & Settlement + C0 Common & Legal)**.

All Office templates are uniformly standardized under the **Empasy 90-8-2 Restrained Consulting Design Standard** (90% pure white and neutral gray, 8% Deep Executive Navy `#0F2547`, 2% Muted Gold `#B08D4E`, single `Malgun Gothic` typeface, Word ISO A4 with headers and footers, Excel freeze panes, A4 print layout, and automated formulas, and PPT 16:9 widescreen), making them immediately ready for monochrome printing and third-party audits across public, financial, and enterprise engagements.
