Scrumban Glossary ​

CategoryTermAbbreviationDescription
Core ConceptsScrumban-An Agile project management framework combining the strengths of Scrum and Kanban
Core ConceptsScrum-An Agile development methodology centered on iterations and sprints
Core ConceptsKanban-A project management technique that visualizes workflow and limits Work in Progress (WIP)
Core ConceptsAgile-A set of philosophies and methodologies for developing products in a flexible, iterative manner
Work UnitsWork Item-A concrete unit of work that the team needs to perform
Work UnitsUser Story-A work unit expressing a user requirement in the form of a concise story
Work UnitsTask-A granular execution item broken down from a User Story or Work Item
Board StructureBacklog-A list of all work items that have not yet been started
Board StructureReady-The status of work items that are fully prepared to begin
Board StructureIn Progress-The status of work currently underway
Board StructureReview-The status of completed work awaiting quality review
Board StructureDone-The status of work that has passed all reviews and is ready for deployment
Operating RulesWork In Progress LimitWIPThe maximum number of work items allowed to be in progress simultaneously
Operating RulesLead Time-The total elapsed time from when a work request is created until it is completed
Operating RulesCycle Time-The elapsed time from when work begins until it is completed
Operating RulesFlow Efficiency-The ratio of active working time to total work elapsed time
EventsDaily Stand-up-A brief daily team meeting to share progress and blockers
EventsSprint Planning Meeting-A meeting to plan the work to be performed in the upcoming sprint
EventsReview Meeting-A meeting to inspect completed work and gather feedback
EventsRetrospective-A recurring team meeting held to improve processes
MetricsBurndown Chart-A chart visualizing remaining work over time
MetricsCumulative Flow DiagramCFDA chart visualizing cumulative item counts by work status to identify bottlenecks
Quality StandardsDefinition of ReadyDoRMinimum specification and acceptance criteria a backlog item must meet before entering development
Quality StandardsDefinition of DoneDoDBuild, QA, and documentation criteria a story or task must pass to be accepted as Done
Estimation TechniquesStory PointSPAn estimation unit representing relative size, complexity, and uncertainty using scales such as the Fibonacci sequence
Estimation TechniquesVelocity-The sum of Story Points completed (Done) by a team during a single sprint
Flow ControlPull System-A flow control method in which the next task is pulled and started only when available capacity (WIP headroom) opens up
Flow ControlBlocker-A bottleneck factor halting work progress due to external dependencies or technical impediments

Relationship Among Core Flow Metrics (Little's Law) ​

In Scrumban operations, lead time is shortened by managing the relationship between Work in Progress (WIP), Throughput, and Cycle Time as follows:

[ \text{Average Cycle Time} = \frac{\text{Average WIP (Work in Progress)}}{\text{Average Throughput (Completed Items per Unit Period)}} ]

  • Effect of WIP Limits: Reducing concurrent work (WIP) lowers context-switching overhead, shortening the completion time (Cycle Time) of individual items and reducing quality defects.
